# Real height across the first-one renewals

Work at the actual odd state $U$ just before the known valuation one
which follows the short maximal run of twos. Both E1 rows have
$U<3N$, checked from the original affine definitions and the exact
book of the valuation-four exit. This proof applies to all five residual
cylinders of the preceding package with no new phase selection.

## One contracting block

An actual word $(1,2,2)$ from $U$ to $W$ gives
$32W=27U+23$, so $W<U$ for $U\ge5$.
Any surplus twos weakly lower height, since an actual edge of valuation
two maps $y$ to $(3y+1)/4\le y$ for positive odd $y$.

At the checkpoint after the first $(1,2)$, use the previously certified
maximal-twos consumer, with the exact original history. Its price is
within the quarter-source window. If the twos continue long enough,
they descend. If their terminal exits with a valuation at least three,
the established quarter-source construction pays. If their terminal
exits with valuation one and has consumed at least one more two, the
new height is strictly smaller and the same rule applies. If the next
valuation after the first one is not two, or a single two is followed by
another one, the first consumer reports that actual frontier.

## Absorbing a single expansion before a contraction

A single block $(1,2)$ raises height, with $8W=9U+5$.
When the next block is contracting, the combined first five valuations
are $(1,2,1,2,2)$ and the exact affine book gives
$$256Z=243U+319.$$
This is a strict drop for $U\ge25$. Additional twos again only lower
height. The state immediately before those additional twos is
$(81U+85)/64$, in the quarter-source window when $U<3N$ and $N\ge32$.
The same physical maximal-twos consumer pays on a strong or long enough
exit. An unpaid next one returns with strictly lower height.

Lean uses strong induction on height. The complete consumer returns
either a physical payment or a reachable cut $V\le U$ that is outside
the grammar. Both the book from $U$ and the separately composed book
from the original $N$ are returned. Thus a tail that remains in the
grammar cannot continue without payment. The grammar allows arbitrary
ordering of contracting renewals and pairs consisting of one
single-dose expansion followed by a contraction.

The residual frontier includes consecutive single-dose expansions
and continuations whose valuation after the first one differs from two.
A frontier at the initial cut need only give equality $V=U$.
Neither the frontier certificate nor the finite witnesses supplies
payment for every fixed seed in the five cylinders.

## Independent physical contrast

For each of the five cylinders, the checker computes the appropriate
finite dyadic refinement without searching a seed interval. It replays
the entire original prefix and both tails. In one tail
$(1,2,1,2,2)$ the intermediate height rises and the final height drops.
In the other $(1,2,1,2)$ the terminal is higher than the entry, with
another real valuation one after it. The latter example refutes
dropping the contraction requirement from the paired height argument.
It does not assert that this seed never pays.
